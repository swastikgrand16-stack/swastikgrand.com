**Product Requirements Document · Version 1.1 · 7 September 2026**  
**Business:** Swastik Grand Industries (SGI)

**Status:** Implementation brief; proposed requirements and copy for owner review. This document does not authorize replacing the live website.

## 1. Purpose

Create a professional, corporate homepage that presents SGI as an industrial cutting-tool manufacturer and custom threading solution provider. The homepage must help a buyer find a suitable product, understand the company’s quality proposition, and enquire about standard, bulk, export or custom requirements.

**Core USP:** Long-life, high-quality cutting tools at affordable prices, supported by practical industrial problem solving.

**Recommended headline:** Better cutting. Longer life. Greater value.

**Supporting copy:** High-quality cutting tools and custom threading solutions that help solve real production challenges—at an affordable price.

Avoid unsupported superlatives, guaranteed tool-life improvements, invented customer counts and unverified delivery promises. Support performance claims with relevant specifications or approved evidence.

## 2. Intended audience and buyer tasks

| Audience | What they need | Homepage route |
|---|---|---|
| Purchase managers / OEM buyers | Product capability, supplier credibility and commercial quotation | Products → product page → bulk enquiry |
| Workshop owners and maintenance teams | Suitable tools for a threading or machining problem | Industrial Solutions → relevant product or enquiry |
| Distributors and wholesalers | Size range, repeat supply and quantity-based pricing | Bulk order enquiry |
| Overseas sourcing teams | Manufacturer information, product specifications and export discussion | Export enquiry → destination and requirements |
| Engineers needing special tools | Feasibility against a drawing or non-standard specification | Custom Taps → drawing-based enquiry |

**Primary conversion:** A qualified product or custom-tool enquiry with usable contact details, size/specification and quantity.

**Secondary conversions:** Product-page visit, phone or WhatsApp conversation, company-profile/ISO request, and factory video view.

## 6. Enquiry functionality

### Required fields

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Enquiry type | Standard / Bulk / Export / Custom; prefilled by originating CTA |
| Name | Required |
| Company | Required for bulk/export/custom |
| Email | Required; valid format |
| Phone / WhatsApp | Required; support international formatting |
| Product or tool requirement | Required; editable |
| Size / thread / specification | Required for product-specific enquiries |
| Quantity | Required for quotation requests; positive integer or a size-wise requirement list |
| Workpiece material | Optional initially; required before a technical recommendation |
| Country / delivery location | Country required for export |
| Required date | Optional; does not create a delivery commitment |
| Additional details | Optional |
| Drawing / photo | Optional for standard; recommended for custom |

Custom prompts: overall length, thread depth, hole type, pitch/TPI, RH/LH, tolerance, coating and drawing reference. Do not force a buyer to know every engineering field; provide “Please recommend” where appropriate.

### Production behaviour

- Validate fields in the browser and on the server.
- Keep entered data after validation or network errors.
- Disable repeat submission while the request is processing.
- Show success only after the backend accepts the enquiry.
- Route accepted leads to an owner-approved company inbox or CRM. No destination is assumed connected by this PRD.
- Include enquiry type, product context and referral page in the lead.
- Provide an explicit retry option and alternate phone/WhatsApp contact on failure.
- Do not promise a response time until SGI commits to one.
- For uploads: proposed limit 10 MB per file; allow PDF, JPG and PNG initially. Validate actual type, restrict access and agree retention with SGI. Request CAD files through the agreed sales channel until supported securely.
- Add a clear enquiry-purpose privacy notice and link to the approved policy. Do not silently subscribe buyers to marketing.

### Prototype behaviour

If email/CRM integration is not yet configured, label the action “Prepare Email Enquiry” or “Open WhatsApp Enquiry”. Tell users to review and send in the destination app. Never show “Enquiry submitted” for merely opening an external app. Drawing attachments are added in the email app unless a real upload service exists.

## 7. Visual and responsive requirements

- Corporate industrial character: deep navy `#14263B`, white `#FFFFFF`, neutral surfaces `#F3F5F7`, and yellow `#F8C52F` for principal actions.
- SGI is the company identity; SWAGIN is the product brand. Use approved logos, not a newly invented permanent brand mark.
- Bold, readable sans-serif headings; body text generally 16–18 px with comfortable line spacing.
- Desktop content width approximately 1280–1440 px; generous but purposeful spacing.
- Product photography on consistent neutral backgrounds. Preserve the entire tool and embedded legitimate branding; do not stretch images.
- Optimise genuine originals for different display sizes. “HD design” means crisp layout and adequate source images, not artificial claims that every old photo is high resolution.
- Desktop: balanced hero with text and product imagery; 3–4 category columns.
- Tablet: 2-column content where readable.
- Mobile: single-column narrative, visible enquiry actions, touch-friendly navigation and no horizontal page scrolling.
- Check 360, 390, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1920 px widths and 200% text zoom.
- No autoplay carousel, intrusive popup or decorative animation that delays access to tools.

## 8. Technical requirements

- Next.js App Router with TypeScript and Tailwind CSS; use compatible stable dependencies chosen at implementation time.
- Reusable components: SiteHeader, Hero, BenefitStrip, ProductCategoryGrid, SolutionsGrid, CustomTapSection, CompanyIntro, QualitySection, VideoSection, OrderOptions, EnquiryForm and SiteFooter.
- Keep company contacts, categories, CTA labels and approved resources in central structured data; avoid duplicating them across components.
- Render core content on the server where practical. Use client components only for interactive navigation, video and forms.
- Use semantic headings, sections, links and buttons. One descriptive H1.
- Set image dimensions; load below-fold images and video lazily. Prioritise the main hero image.
- Keep secrets server-side; do not expose mail-provider or CRM keys in browser code.
- Choose hosting compatible with the selected Next.js features. Treat domain cutover as a separate launch action.
- Provide source code, dependency lockfile, environment-variable example without secrets, and setup/deployment instructions.

## 10. Measurement and acceptance

Track only non-sensitive interaction metadata. Do not send names, email addresses, phone numbers or drawing content to analytics.

Suggested events: `product_category_click`, `custom_enquiry_start`, `bulk_enquiry_start`, `export_enquiry_start`, `enquiry_submit_success`, `enquiry_submit_error`, `whatsapp_click`, `phone_click`, `video_play` and `resource_download`. A WhatsApp click or email draft is not a submitted lead.

Measure qualified enquiries, form completion, product discovery and enquiry type. Establish a baseline after launch before setting a business conversion target.
